GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before you begin.

What can I do in NextSDE?

You can practice coding questions, study interactive courses, work through low level design problems, and prepare a resume.

Can I choose a specific interview topic?

Yes. Practice questions can be filtered by topic, difficulty, and company. Courses are organized by subject.

Do I need to follow a fixed path?

No. Start with the skill that matters most for your next interview and move between sections as needed.

Does coding practice include feedback?

The practice workspace includes an editor and test cases so you can check your solution as you work.
